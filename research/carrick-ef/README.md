# C1: Carrick e and f from the production sheets

**Decision: accept both.** Carrick's e and f were the last letters still traced from the flyer, because
they touch each other on N513, N47 and N45. On NIVAL N514's two production sheets they are printed many
times, isolated. Carrick Study 3 ([round 6](../round-06/README.md)) redraws them from the sheets' means.

| | Source | Impressions | Spread | Cross-check | Topology |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| e | 60 pt sheet (S53914) | 24: 14 e, and the bodies of 10 é | 0.068 | 36 pt mean of 32: rms 0.039 to the 60 pt mean | 2 pieces: the lower wedge is detached in 11 of 24 impressions and on N513 |
| f | 60 pt sheet | 6 | 0.032 | 36 pt mean of 7: rms 0.059 | 1 piece in all 13 |

The é bodies vary between one piece and two exactly as the e's do (row by row), so they are the same
drawing and are averaged with the e. Each letter keeps the flyer trace's size and place (frame lock); only
its shape comes from the sheet.

Rejected: the flyer traces (97 dpi; the e/f pair is fused on every larger source) and a division of N513's
fused pair, which would leave the contact a construction where the sheets show both letters whole.

Every impression's box, affine transform, spread and topology is in [evidence.json](evidence.json), in the
photograph's native pixels (SHA-256 `525f4bb849a7f5bea66f82ca5aad0f2dbb3a9d8d7257ae2e91ed7e36c08ef35d`). The
crops and means hold archive pixels and stay in ignored `build/production/carrick/`.
