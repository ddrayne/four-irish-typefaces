FONTLOG for Four Irish Typefaces
================================

This file provides detailed information on the Four Irish Typefaces font software.


Basic font information
----------------------

Céilí, Carrick, Kilfane and Tuam Uncial won the 1978 "Design an Irish Typeface" competition
sponsored by Letraset, Kilkenny Design Workshops and D. O'Sullivan Graphic Supplies, and were sold
as Letraset dry-transfer sheets. These fonts, Céilí Digital, Carrick Digital, Kilfane Digital and
Tuam Uncial Digital, are independent digitisations of them, not the original type. They were digitised in 2026, first from a
scan of the competition's "Four Irish Typefaces" flyer and, since 3.000, from the original specimens
and production sheets in the National Irish Visual Arts Library; they are completed to about 400
characters each, and extended with styles the originals never had. See README.md for how they were made and how faithful they
are, and docs/validation/ for the evidence.

  Céilí        Hermann Hecht, 1978 (first prize, £250 and a silver medallion)
  Carrick      William Curran, 1978 (Highly Commended, £50)
  Kilfane      William Curran, 1978 (Commended, £25)
  Tuam Uncial  Jarlath Hayes, 1978 (Commended, £25)

The award wording follows the competition brochure in the William Curran Archive at the National
Irish Visual Arts Library (https://nival.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/collection/p21086coll129/id/45/).
It prints Céilí's name as "Caill".

No copyright is claimed in the fonts. To the extent possible under law, Dan Drayne has waived all
copyright and related or neighboring rights to the digitisation, and dedicates it to the public
domain under CC0 1.0 Universal (LICENSE.txt). The 1978 designs are the work of their designers,
named above.


ChangeLog
---------

3.100 (2026-10-27)
- Finishing passes on every face after independent type reviews. Outlines are settled on the unit
  grid with smooth joins, nodes at extremes and no slivers; each correction and its reason is in
  research/ (each face's judged-edits.json and README).
- Kilfane: the letters on one set of lines and stems evened. The Bold and Bold Italic draw their
  accents, rules, math signs, bullet, % ‰, small figures and the letters in @ © ® with the bold's
  pen. Their curves (G S s v w x y ß W) are true curves, the joins of k r 6 9 and the g's tail stay
  open, the dots of ! ? i j stand clear, and the cedilla and ogonek take the bold's weight. Capitals
  keep 30 units from capitals and from lowercase (20 before lowercase in the bolds), and 40 before
  an accented i. The bolds' j is spaced as i, and ? as ! in every style. A case feature sets dashes,
  guillemets, colon, bullets, @ and ¡ ¿ for capitals (CSS: font-feature-settings: "case").
- Carrick: f on the baseline; the diaeresis is N513's paired dots; % ‰ # ¤ @ in the figures' weight,
  and ™'s small m with its cuts open; ¢'s bar clear of the counter; the figures on one line; ḃ's
  ponc beside its ascender, ḋ's and ḟ's well above theirs (b, d, f + U+0307 compose to them); £ and
  $ on the baseline at their printed heights; ‹ › « » one drawing and its mirror image; f clear of a
  following ascender; ' and " as deep as the curly quotes. The Italic keeps the Regular's cuts and
  counters.
- Tuam Uncial: the arch cuts, slits and seams at the widths N45 prints; stems evened; dots and
  accents at N45's gaps; í's fada steeper, so í and i are told apart at text sizes; ḃ's and ḋ's ponc
  beside the ascender, ḟ's above; a heavier comma, quotes and apostrophe, and ' " to match; § in
  continuous curves; % ‰ from its own 0. The Italic has the Regular's weight and cuts. The word
  space (and the no-break space) is half the n, 448 (Italic 435), not 320.
- Céilí: the h on the baseline; stems evened; pen terminals as straight cuts; the dots of ! ? are
  the period's pen diamond; l' L' ł' kern by one rule with the other quote pairs; ð and đ spaced as
  d; € and ¥ on the figures' line; h's foot straight and j's bar as i's. The Bold and Bold Italic
  keep the Regular's dot gaps and open joins (b d k p r 9); their heavy level strokes (7 3, dashes,
  + = #), math signs, bullet, % ‰ and the letters of @ © ® take the bold's weight.
- All four faces: the brackets, braces and bar stand with each face's parentheses; #'s bars are
  centred on its sloped strokes; ≈ is drawn at the weight and spacing of =; the rings of @ and ¤ are
  at least half the weight of the o, © and ® keep the figures' height with heavier rings, and ° is
  heavier with its counter open, no taller than 0.3 of the figures. Combining marks stand clear of
  their letters, stack on one another and follow a precomposed letter's accent; the dot above keeps
  the ponc's place, so Irish ḃ ċ ḋ ḟ ġ ṁ ṗ ṡ ṫ are unchanged, and the comma below hangs clear of a
  descender.
- The line box changes in Kilfane (999/-214 to 1023/-204), Carrick (965/-235 to 988/-213) and Tuam
  Uncial (936/-264 to 951/-258), for taller accents and the bolds' cedillas; Céilí's is unchanged.
  The TrueType files keep no speck where a tiny curve's conversion crossed itself, and the fonts
  build byte for byte the same on any machine.
- Names are unchanged; coverage adds U+0323 (combining dot below); features add mkmk (and Kilfane's
  case).

3.000 (2026-10-26)
- The fonts are redrawn from the 1978 originals in the National Irish Visual Arts Library (NIVAL),
  not the photocopied flyer: Kilfane from the N516 alphabet specimen and the averaged impressions of
  its Letraset production sheet; Carrick from the N513 specimen and the averaged impressions of its
  two production sheets, each letter set in the sheet's own frame by Letraset's spacing marks; Tuam
  Uncial and Céilí from the mean of the flyer (N46) and the catalogue (N45), with Céilí's
  disagreements decided by a third print (N44). Each study's evidence is in research/.
- Tuam Uncial's braces are drawn with a point; Kilfane's and Carrick's stem feet sit on the baseline.
- Names, coverage and features are unchanged; spacing follows the new outlines.

2.101 (2026-10-25)
- No copyright is claimed: the fonts are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal
  (LICENSE.txt) instead of being licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The name table's
  copyright, licence and licence URL say so. Outlines, spacing, features and names are unchanged.
- The fonts are downloaded from the project's GitHub releases. The site no longer serves the fonts or
  the stylesheet to other sites (no cross-origin access): host your own copy.

2.100 (2026-10-25)
- The families are renamed Céilí Digital, Carrick Digital, Kilfane Digital and Tuam Uncial Digital
  (files CeiliDigital-Regular.otf and so on), so that the fonts are never taken for the original
  type. The description names the design and says the font is an independent digitisation.
  Outlines, spacing and features are unchanged. Update font-family names in CSS and documents.

2.010 (2026-10-25)
- The award wording corrected from the competition brochure: Carrick was Highly Commended and
  Kilfane and Tuam Uncial Commended, not second, joint third and fourth. It is in each font's
  description field, so the fonts are reissued; outlines, spacing and features are unchanged.

2.000 (2026-10-19)
- Families: an italic for every face, and a bold and bold italic for Céilí and Kilfane, drawn from
  the regulars (a wider pen in each face's contrast; a narrowed, sloped roman). Style-linked
  (name table, OS/2 and head style bits, STAT), with shared line metrics per family.
- Céilí: hairlines the scan lost restored in b, e and e.alt1.
- The section sign redrawn in every face; Tuam Uncial's © and ® letters kept clear of the ring.

1.200 (2026-10)
- Every glyph on the flyer audited against the scan (224 glyphs, mean overlap 0.948).
- Kilfane's and Carrick's accents taken from the accent sets printed on the flyer; the private-use
  "ornament" removed. Céilí's h rebuilt from more of the flyer's drawing; Tuam Uncial's fadas set
  as on the flyer; smaller fixes; 1.2 em default line height.

1.100 (2026-10)
- Every traced letter redrawn: true straight stems and bars, smooth curves with few points,
  sharp corners. Céilí's h rebuilt; a toner speck removed from Kilfane's O.

1.000 (2026-10)
- First release: the four regulars, about 400 characters each, kerning, mark positioning and the
  sheets' alternates as stylistic sets.
