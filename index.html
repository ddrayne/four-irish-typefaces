four irish typefaces

Céilí, Carrick, Kilfane and Tuam Uncial won the 1978 “Design an Irish Typeface” competition sponsored by Letraset, Kilkenny Design Workshops and D. O’Sullivan Graphic Supplies, and were sold as Letraset dry-transfer sheets. These independent digital versions, named Céilí Digital, Carrick Digital, Kilfane Digital and Tuam Uncial Digital to keep them apart from the originals, were traced from the competition’s “Four Irish Typefaces” flyer and completed with around 400 characters each, covering Irish (including traditional lenition dots and ⁊), Scottish Gaelic, Welsh and the other Latin-script languages of Europe.

The sheet shows each face in one style. Version 2 adds, as new work drawn in 2026 from those regulars, an italic for all four and a bold and bold italic for Céilí and Kilfane. Carrick and Tuam Uncial are already black.
